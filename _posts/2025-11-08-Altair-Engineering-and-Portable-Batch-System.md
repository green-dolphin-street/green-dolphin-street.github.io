# Altair Engineering
* A multinational technology company that provides software and cloud solutions.
### Software & Resources on Altair One:
    * **Simulation:** A broad suite of tools (like **Altair HyperWorks**) for structural analysis, fluid dynamics (CFD), electromagnetics, and more.
    * **Data Analytics & AI:** A platform (like **Altair RapidMiner**) for data science, machine learning, and AI-driven insights.
    * **HPC & Cloud:** Tools to manage HPC workloads (including **PBS Professional**), submit jobs, and access scalable computing resources on the cloud.
    * 
### **Altair One:**
    * Altair's unified cloud platform and "Gateway."
    * It's a single point of access for customers to use Altair's entire suite of software, manage licenses (using Altair Units), access cloud-based HPC resources, and collaborate.

# PBS (Portable Batch System):
    * A software suite for workload management and job scheduling, essential for managing tasks on high-performance computing (HPC) clusters. It queues jobs, allocates resources (like CPUs/GPUs), and manages their execution.
    * Originally developed for NASA under a contract project that began on 1991.
    * Altair Engineering acquired the rights to all the PBS technology in 2003. It currently owns and maintains the intellectual property associated with PBS, and also employs the original development team from NASA.
    * Now, there are OpenPBS and PBS Professional, an open-source and licensed versions of PBS.
