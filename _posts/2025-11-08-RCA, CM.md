### RCA (Root Cause Analysis)

* **Timing:** Post-Incident (Reactive)
* **Purpose:** Prevent problem recurrence.
* **Key Action:** Investigate and identify the fundamental cause of a failure.
* **Output:** RCA Report.

---

### CM (Change Management)

* **Timing:** Pre-Change (Proactive)
* **Purpose:** Minimize service disruption and risk from changes.
* **Key Action:** Plan, approve, and test (Preflight) changes.
* **Output:** CM Plan (including Rollback Plan).
