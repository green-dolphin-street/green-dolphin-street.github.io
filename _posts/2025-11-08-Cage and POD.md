### Cage
* **Definition:** A physical security barrier, typically a wire-mesh fence or partition.
* **Purpose:** **Security & Access Control**. Used to physically secure a tenant's racks from others.
* **Common Use:** Co-location (multi-tenant) data centers.
* **Scope:** An enclosure *around* one or more racks.

---

### POD (Point of Deployment)

* **Definition:** A logical, standardized, and repeatable "building block" of infrastructure.
* **Purpose:** **Scalability & Standardization**. Used to rapidly deploy new capacity.
* **Common Use:** Large-scale cloud and enterprise data centers.
* **Scope:** A pre-defined set *of* racks, network switches, servers, and power. (e.g., "1 POD = 20 Racks + 2 Leaf Switches").

---

### Summary: Cage vs. POD

| Attribute | Cage | POD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Concept** | Physical Security | Logical Design |
| **Main Goal** | Access Control | Scalability |
| **Represents** | A "pen" or secure area | A "stamp" or "Lego block" |
| **Context** | Co-location / Multi-Tenant | Architecture / Deployment |
